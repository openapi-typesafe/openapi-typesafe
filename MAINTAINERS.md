## Original project

project was created by Drew Powers and built by more than 230 contributors; `openapi-react-query` was created by
Martin Paucot. They are not maintainers of this fork, and their copyright notices are kept in [LICENSE](./LICENSE).
The full upstream contributor history is preserved in git.
